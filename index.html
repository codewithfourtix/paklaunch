How to get involved

Building in Lahore?
Start here.

In April 2020, Aly Fahd started Paklaunch as a WhatsApp group to connect Pakistani entrepreneurs with investors and mentors. It grew into an international network. The Lahore chapter is a place to meet other people building in the city and tap into that wider community.
